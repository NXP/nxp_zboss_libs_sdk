Trace format constants for serial trace.

  • FMT__P – for pointer
  • FMT__D – for decimal (like d) or for hexadecimal (like x) integer
  • FMT__L – for long decimal (like ld) or for hexadecimal (like lx) integer
  • FMT__A – for 8 byte array
  • FMT__H – for short decimal (like hd) or for hexadecimal (like hx) integer
  • FMT__C – for char
  • FMT__0 – for no argument

Other format constants are just combinations of ones mentioned below.
